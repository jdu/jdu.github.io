= Remove more than you add

One of the themes throughout your outline is that organisations become buried beneath accumulated decisions.

Yet none of the Core Moves explicitly says

Delete things.

Imagine a chapter like this.

Signal:

Every project leaves behind more than it removes.

Principle:

The highest leverage architectural work is often subtraction.

Practice:

For every system, process, integration, report or concept you introduce, identify one that should disappear.

Pitfall:

Treating architecture as continual construction.

Takeaway:

Healthy organisations don't just accumulate capability.

They continuously shed obsolete understanding.
